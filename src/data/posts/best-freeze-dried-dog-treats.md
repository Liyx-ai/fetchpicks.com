### How to Choose Freeze-Dried Treats

1. **Single vs blended** - Single-ingredient (Vital Essentials) suits allergies; blends (Stella & Chewy's) add balance. Match to your dog's needs.
2. **Training size** - Look for small, breakable pieces for high-rep training. Big chunks are better as occasional rewards.
3. **Protein source** - Novel proteins (rabbit, quail) help bored or allergic dogs. Common proteins (chicken, beef) are cheapest.
4. **Sourcing transparency** - Brands like Open Farm publish farm sources. Higher trust, slightly higher price.
5. **Moderation** - Freeze-dried is rich and calorie-dense. Count it toward daily calories to avoid weight gain.

## FAQ

**Are freeze-dried treats raw?**

They're raw meat with water removed (not cooked). Handle like raw: wash hands, store sealed, and don't leave out in heat. Safe for most dogs to eat.

**Can freeze-dried treats replace meals?**

No - they're treats (often lacking complete nutrition). Use for training and rewards, not as a diet. Count toward daily calories.

**Why are they more expensive?**

Freeze-drying is energy-intensive and uses real meat with little filler. You're paying for nutrient retention and pure ingredients, not starch.
