### How to Brush Your Dog's Teeth

1. **Start slow** - Let your dog lick paste off your finger, then a finger brush, then a real brush over a week. Never force it.
2. **Enzymatic matters** - Enzymatic pastes (Virbac, Petrodex) work even with imperfect technique by breaking down plaque chemically.
3. **Reach the molars** - Dental disease starts at the back. Use an angled or dual-headed brush to hit the far teeth.
4. **Never human toothpaste** - Fluoride and xylitol are toxic to dogs. Only use pet-specific paste.
5. **Daily is ideal, every other day works** - Even 3x/week slows disease substantially versus never.

## FAQ

**How often should I brush my dog's teeth?**

Daily is ideal; every other day is the minimum to be effective. Even 3 times a week dramatically cuts plaque versus never brushing.

**Can I use human toothpaste?**

Never. Human toothpaste contains fluoride and often xylitol, both toxic to dogs. Use only pet-formulated paste (safe to swallow).

**My dog won't tolerate a toothbrush - what then?**

Start with a finger brush (Vet's Best) or a water additive (TropiClean) for maintenance. A vet dental cleaning under anesthesia may be needed if disease is advanced.
