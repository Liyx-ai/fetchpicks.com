## How to Choose Dog Food for a French Bulldog

1. **Skip chicken if they itch** - Chicken is the #1 allergen in Frenchies. If your dog scratches or has chronic ear issues, try salmon, lamb, or duck.
2. **Watch calories closely** - Frenchies gain weight fast and extra pounds worsen their breathing. Use the bag's chart for the dog's *target* weight, not current weight.
3. **Small kibble beats big kibble** - Flat muzzles make big kibble hard to pick up. Look for small, flat-shaped pieces.
4. **Address the stomach** - Look for highly digestible proteins, prebiotics, or probiotics if your Frenchie has loose stool or gas.
5. **Wet food helps hydration** - Frenchies drink awkwardly. Adding wet food or a topper helps prevent urinary issues.

## FAQ

**Why is my Frenchie always itchy?**

Most commonly a food allergy (chicken is the usual suspect), environmental allergies, or yeast in the skin folds. Try a salmon-based food for 4-6 weeks before assuming it's environmental.

**How much should a French Bulldog eat per day?**

A 20-28lb adult Frenchie needs about 1 to 1.5 cups of dry food per day, split into two meals. Adjust by body condition - you should be able to feel ribs under a thin layer of fat.

**Do French Bulldogs need grain-free food?**

No. Grain-free isn't necessary unless your vet confirms a grain allergy, and it carries a DCM risk in some breeds. Most Frenchies do fine with rice or barley.

**Should I feed my Frenchie wet or dry food?**

Dry food helps clean teeth and is more affordable. Wet food helps hydration. The best approach is usually a quality dry food with a wet food topper.
