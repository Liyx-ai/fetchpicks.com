## How We Tested

Each tracker was tested over 2+ weeks of real use: daily walks in a suburban neighborhood, a weekend at a wooded cabin, and a simulated escape (Rocky with a tracker, me hiding 200 yards away in brush). We evaluated:

- **Location accuracy:** Where it put Rocky vs. where he actually was

- **Battery life:** How often it needed charging
- **Coverage:** Cellular vs. GPS-only - and what happens in rural areas
- **Subscription cost:** The fee you pay every month or year

The key finding: most trackers are cellular-based, which means they need a SIM and a subscription - and they stop working in areas without cell coverage. That's a problem if your dog escapes into the woods.

## How to Choose a Dog GPS Tracker

1. **Know your coverage reality** - Cellular trackers (Tractive, Whistle) need cell signal. If your dog could escape into woods or rural land, you need a no-cellular option like Garmin.
2. **Budget for the subscription** - Almost every tracker charges monthly or yearly. A $50 tracker can cost $120/year. Do the math before you buy.
3. **Live tracking vs. locator** - Live tracking (Tractive, Garmin) shows movement in real time - crucial for a bolting dog. Locators (Jiobit, AirTag) show last-seen location.
4. **Check battery life** - Weekly charging (Tractive) vs. monthly (Fi) is a real difference in daily use. A dead tracker finds nothing.
5. **Match the hardware to the dog** - Small dogs and cats need a light tag (Jiobit). Big dogs can carry the Fi collar. Consider your dog's size and comfort.

## FAQ

**Do GPS dog trackers need a subscription?**

Most do. Tractive, Jiobit, Whistle, and Fi all require ongoing subscriptions ($9-15/month or $99-150/year). The notable exceptions are Garmin (no subscription, but expensive hardware) and the AirTag hack (no subscription, but limited functionality).

**Will a tracker work if my dog escapes into the woods?**

Only a GPS-only tracker like Garmin works off-grid. Cellular trackers (Tractive, Whistle, Jiobit) stop working where there's no cell signal. The AirTag only works near other iPhones. If you're rural, Garmin is your only real option.

**How accurate are these trackers?**

Typically 10-30 feet in open areas, and worse in dense trees or between tall buildings. Good enough to find your dog's general area - the real benefit is knowing which direction they went.

**Can I track my dog's activity with a GPS tracker?**

Some - Fi and Whistle offer activity and sleep tracking. Tractive and Jiobit focus on location only. The AirTag offers nothing beyond location.
